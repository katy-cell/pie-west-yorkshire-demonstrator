# Clinical and pathway logic

National guidance is used to establish the *review domains*, not to invent local operational thresholds.

- **NICE NG136**: hypertension diagnosis, treatment, monitoring and cardiovascular-risk review.
- **NICE NG196**: atrial fibrillation, including stroke/bleeding risk and personalised care.
- **NICE NG106**: chronic heart failure, including multidisciplinary working and medication monitoring.
- **NICE NG249**: falls assessment and prevention for older people and people aged 50+ at higher risk.
- **NHS England virtual wards framework**: hospital-at-home operating context.
- **NHS England neighbourhood health guidance**: population health management, MDT coordination, linked data and proactive care.

Local formularies, escalation criteria and pathway time standards should be held as versioned local rules after confirmation with the named clinical and service owners. The demonstrator therefore shows a review opportunity and suggested action domain, not a medication instruction or clinical threshold.
