# AI capability: ambitious, explainable and grounded

## What the demonstrator shows

- **Operational risk detection**: missing ownership, overdue action, low source freshness and incomplete person communication.
- **Guideline-informed review opportunity**: transparent features such as recent discharge, monitoring change, heart-failure context, falls history and medicines-review timing.
- **Demand forecasting**: a projected workload of people whose modelled review opportunity crosses a chosen threshold.
- **Intervention simulation**: the expected change to the synthetic queue when handover acceptance, targeted monitoring or review capacity improves.
- **AI synthesis**: concise, traceable case and pathway explanations for a team review.

## What it does not do

It does not diagnose, prescribe, autonomously triage or claim a clinically validated prediction of hospital admission. Any production clinical model would need local dataset definition, external validation, fairness testing, clinical safety review, monitoring and a human decision-maker.
