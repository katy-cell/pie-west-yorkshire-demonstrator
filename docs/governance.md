# Governance and safety boundary

## Demonstrator boundary

- Synthetic people, pathway events, alerts, timestamps and operational clocks only.
- No patient-identifiable, confidential, sensitive or live operational data.
- No credentials, API keys, tokens or production integration configuration.
- No autonomous decision-making or clinical recommendation.
- No claim of access to NHS systems, shared records, telecare platforms or local-authority systems.

## Future pilot boundary

A real deployment would require an agreed purpose, roles and responsibilities, lawful basis, data-sharing route, information-governance review, privacy assessment where appropriate, cyber assurance, local technical onboarding, clinical safety work where applicable, auditability and human oversight.

## Responsible SDOH use

Area-level deprivation, digital access and demographic context can help services adapt an offer. They should not be treated as a deterministic individual risk score or substitute for professional and person-led assessment.
